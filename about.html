We're a small, focused team of strategists and creatives who believe every business deserves a social media presence that generates real results.

Our Story

Founded on Results

The Viral Bubble was born from a simple frustration: too many businesses were paying agencies for followers and likes that never translated into revenue. We set out to build something different — an agency that measures success the same way our clients do: by the leads generated and the revenue earned.

Founded by Genisha Dassanayake, The Viral Bubble has grown into a trusted partner for businesses across Australia. From local tradies to national e-commerce brands, our clients stay because the numbers speak for themselves.

What We Believe

Results Over Vanity

Followers mean nothing if they don't convert. Every strategy we build is reverse-engineered from the outcome that matters to your business — whether that's leads, sales or bookings.

Craft Over Volume

We'd rather produce three pieces of content that resonate than thirty that scroll past. Quality compounds. Volume dilutes.

Partnership Over Service

You'll never speak to a different person every call. Your dedicated strategist knows your brand intimately and is accountable for your growth.
