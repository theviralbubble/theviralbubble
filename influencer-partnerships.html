Influencer Partnerships

We connect your brand with creators who share your values and speak to your audience — then manage the entire partnership so you don't have to.

The right voices, not the loudest

Follower count is a vanity metric. What matters is whether a creator's audience trusts them, engages with their content and overlaps with the people you want to reach. A well-matched micro-influencer often delivers stronger results than a celebrity post that disappears into a feed of millions.

We research, vet and shortlist creators based on audience quality, content style, engagement patterns and brand alignment. Every recommendation is backed by data and shaped by experience — not pulled from a marketplace.

Once you approve the shortlist, we handle everything: outreach, negotiation, contracts, creative briefing, content review, scheduling and post-campaign reporting. You stay informed at every stage without managing any of it.

What's Included

Creator Sourcing

Hand-curated shortlists of vetted influencers matched to your brand, audience and campaign goals. Every creator is assessed for audience authenticity, engagement quality and content alignment.

Campaign Management

End-to-end execution — from negotiation and contracts to creative briefing, content approval, publishing schedules and performance tracking. You approve; we deliver.

Performance Reporting

Detailed post-campaign analytics covering reach, engagement, clicks and conversions. Every report includes recommendations for future partnerships based on what worked.

UGC & Licensing

We negotiate content usage rights so you can repurpose influencer-created content across your own channels, ads and website — extending the value of every partnership beyond a single post.
